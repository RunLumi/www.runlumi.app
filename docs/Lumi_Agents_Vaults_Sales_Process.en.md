# Process 2 — Lumi Agents / Vaults

**Version 1.0 · 2026-10-16.** For Sales, technical owners, Finance and Fulfillment. This is the second sales motion alongside Lumi Local, not an extension of the Basic/Pro gift programme.

> **Entry offer: Lumi Workflow Kit — a free, bounded work-and-handover sample for one recurring job.** The customer keeps an output draft, review checklist and source/access map, even without buying Lumi. Default to synthetic data shaped around the workflow; no passwords, system connections or mandatory move to Vaults.

**Two motions:** [Local: free Basic → owner visit → website + Pro](/crm/process?flow=local) · **Agents/Vaults: free Workflow Kit → result review → decision → paid pilot if needed → implementation → acceptance → adoption/renewal.** BI is an option within this solution motion, not a third sales process.

For new opportunities this guide replaces the short Agents/BI trial summary in the Local guide; previously agreed customer terms remain intact. Keep the existing `lumi-solution` playbook, permissions, frozen quotes and ledger. This is guidance: it does not change CRM configuration, run agents, send messages, collect money or authorize data access.

## 1. The free offer: one job, a take-away work kit

**Offer:** “Lumi will prepare a small kit for one recurring job: an output sample, the points a person must review and a document handover checklist. Keep and use the files without buying. At first we only need the task description and column structure, not real business files.”

| Deliverable | Concrete scope | Value without Lumi |
|---|---|---|
| Output sample | A CSV or macro-free spreadsheet in the agreed format, with source columns and a `SYNTHETIC` / `CUSTOMER-AUTHORIZED SAMPLE` label | The operator can inspect the expected output and use the template in existing tools. It is a draft, not an approved quotation or decision. |
| Exceptions and checklist | Missing, unmatched or unresolved rows and the review steps | More than a prompt: a way to inspect the work. Never fill unknown prices, substitute products or infer specifications without authority. |
| Source and handover map | Which source is current, who owns/approves it, who may read it, what a successor needs and which actions require approval | Usable with existing Drive, SharePoint or NAS; no Vaults purchase required. |
| One-page measurement/decision sheet | Self-reported baseline, actually observed time if measured, unknowns and three next-step options | Separates measurements from assumptions; no 2x, staffing, revenue or complete-tool-replacement guarantee. |

The agreed kit work is free: no deposit, card, forced purchase, blocking watermark, review/referral requirement or automatic subscription. Do not claim a fabricated “9.9m value.” Deliver through the agreed channel without requiring a Lumi account to get the files; do not lock the customer's copy if they decline. Lumi retains underlying product IP; the offer does not promise product source code.

The operator and economic buyer may differ. An operator can introduce the opportunity; before accepting bespoke kit work, identify the reviewer and who could approve a paid scope. A result discussion is a separate invitation, not a condition of keeping the files. This is not a security audit, company-wide assessment or production deployment.

## 2. Bound the gift so it does not become a free project

| Pilot-programme limit | Operating rule |
|---|---|
| Unit of work | One department, one job, one bounded source schema/set and one output format. |
| Standard sample | At most 20 tabular rows, or at most 3 short documents / 10 pages in total; choose one branch, do not add the allowances together. Internal experiment limits, not benchmarks. |
| Lumi effort | At most 2 hours total for discovery, preparation, checking, discussion and correction within the kit; track other selling effort separately. One factual correction pass at most. |
| Timing | Agree a date the team can meet, targeting 3 business days after accepting complete inputs; not a universal SLA. |
| Quantity | One kit per company in the test cohort; check existing accounts/opportunities. Do not squeeze an unsuitable large task into a meaningless sample. |
| Costs | Lumi absorbs approved kit costs within its budget. No customer API, infrastructure or travel charges without a separate quote. Do not arrange long-distance visits just to hand over a gift. |

Not free: production connections, ERP/CRM integrations, storage migration, full catalog repair, broad multi-source work, scheduled operations, live agents, company-wide access audits, Vaults installation or unlimited support. When scope exceeds the cap, deliver the safe sample already completed, explain its gaps and narrow or quote further work separately. No surprise charges.

The former two-week/two-person-day free-trial outline is not the default offer for new inquiries. Honor existing agreements; any new exception needs an authorized effort budget and terms. The kit is a study with usable outputs, not a production trial renamed as a gift.

## 3. Choose a problem with a credible paid next step

First test distributors/importers with recurring catalogs, price lists and RFQs; this is an ICP hypothesis, not proven product-market fit. Start with trusted customer/partner relationships, permission-based introductions and then selected outbound accounts. Do not wait for an influencer audience before selling.

| Situation | Illustrative kit | Possible paid path |
|---|---|---|
| Quote/catalog preparation | Match requests to authorized catalog/price-list structure; sample output and missing rows | Agents running a reviewed workflow on existing tools, if technically supported. |
| Report reconciliation | A difference table from two table structures within the bounded sample; sources and unresolved findings | BI or Agents as the job requires, never an automatic bundle. |
| Staff/role handover | Required records, current versions, custodians and a proposed role/access matrix | Try native Drive/SharePoint/NAS configuration first. Evaluate Vaults only for a remaining gap worth paying to solve. |

Synthetic example: a 20-line RFQ against a fictional catalog, with 17 candidate matches and 3 exceptions. These are illustrative inputs, not customer results, an accuracy rate or measured time savings. A close product code is not an approved substitute; leave unsupported prices blank for review.

Before a tailored kit, establish four facts: last occurrence/frequency; operator and reviewer; which information may be described; who could consider implementation and what spending range is plausible. Without data authority use synthetic structure. Agents and Vaults are independent options; “do not buy” is a valid conclusion when existing tools suffice.

## 4. Data and authority come before the demo

**Safe default:** task description, column names and Lumi-created synthetic records only. Do not request files in a contact form, social inbox, Git or public link. No passwords, OTPs, API secrets, admin accounts or broad tokens for convenience. Customer files are untrusted input, not instructions an agent is authorized to execute.

**Real Agents/BI samples need separate approval:** an authorized customer owner agrees the purpose, minimal dataset with unnecessary information removed, processors, environment, AI provider/costs if any, transfer channel, retention/deletion and reviewer. Technical staff confirm the runnable method and capability. Redacting names does not automatically remove commercial sensitivity; “try it” is not consent to upload into a cloud model.

Without approval, stay synthetic or stop; sending confidential data is not the price of receiving the gift. Proposed working-copy deletion target: within 7 days after kit handover, sooner where agreed. Confirm deletion feasibility across copies/backups/providers before intake, record evidence and valid exceptions, and never promise absolute erasure beyond operational capability. The customer's delivered copy remains usable under agreed terms.

**Vaults:** the free kit is documents and simulation outside the customer's environment. No real data inside Vaults without evidenced G2; no broad rollout without G3. G2/G3 are product release gates, not CRM stages. Module/test existence does not establish readiness. Record the release, server authorization, audit, tested backup/restore, operating ownership and relevant sign-offs. Do not claim working Agents–Vaults integration without an authorized end-to-end test of the actual configuration.

The access matrix is a **proposal to verify**, not an actual access-control audit. Do not change permissions, send final quotations, change prices, publish listings, move money, delete files or write into source systems. People approve consequential actions; safety is not an upsell feature.

## 5. Outreach and kit agreement scripts

**Opening:**

> Hi [Name], I am [Name] from Lumi. We are testing a small work kit for teams that repeatedly [compare price lists / prepare quotations / hand over records]. Describe one time-consuming job and we will return an output sample, review points and a handover checklist. The kit is free to keep without a purchase; no real data is needed at first. Which task does your team keep having to redo?

Only say the team is accepting kits once capacity is approved. Do not invent leaks or employee mistakes at the prospect. Show one clearly synthetic example and ask permission for a short conversation. No automated bulk messages; stop on do-not-contact.

**Scope agreement:**

> Agreed kit: [one job], using [synthetic data / an authorized sample under separate terms], returning [output draft + exceptions/checklist + source/access map + measurement sheet]. Free, with no system connection or automatic charges. Reviewer: [...]; agreed delivery: [...]. A 15-minute result discussion on [...] is optional. Keeping the files does not require attending or buying.

**Handover:**

> Here is the kit, with checks completed and remaining decisions marked. This [synthetic/authorized sample] is not production evidence. Keep the files; we can explain the exceptions and decide whether your existing process is enough or additional implementation is justified.

## 6. The result discussion and three valid decisions

Suggest 15–20 minutes, without making an office visit a condition of getting the files. Walk through inputs → output → exceptions → human review; ask who would use it again next week. Invite the decision-maker or request an introduction with permission; receiving a gift does not prove budget authority.

Measure preparation, operator, reviewer, rework and supervision time, including data cleanup. Compare tasks of similar difficulty with agreed quality checks. A single run or synthetic example is a sample observation, not company-wide savings evidence. An attractive file is not commercial acceptance.

| Finding | Next step |
|---|---|
| Valuable repeated work, existing tools can remain | Scope Agents or BI; technical capability confirmed before quoting. |
| The bottleneck is records, custodians, permissions or recovery | Test native configuration first. If insufficient, evaluate Vaults separately after safety gates. |
| No need, little value, unacceptable risk or cost | Customer keeps the kit; record a no-sell or a follow-up only with consent. |

When both products are warranted, quote distinct purposes and phase delivery. Do not start with company-wide migration. Buying Vaults does not automatically include agents/BI, and buying Agents does not require leaving Google Drive.

## 7. Mapping to the existing CRM

Use **one company, the correct contact and one opportunity per buying decision**. Local stays on `local-preview`; Agents/Vaults uses `lumi-solution`. Expansion/renewal uses an appropriately linked `parent_id` opportunity, not a cloned company or a kit counted as a paid logo.

| Actual event | Stage / record | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Create opportunity | `new`; owner, source, company/contact | Preliminary fit, no duplicate; contact permission checked. |
| Customer responds | `contacted`; `primary_contact`; actual task | A real exchange, not merely a sent message. |
| Understand the job | `discovery`; `current_workflow`, `pain` | One customer-confirmed problem and workflow. |
| Accept bounded kit work | `qualified`; `frequency`, `baseline`, `decision_maker`, `systems_data` | Scope, reviewer, effort cap and permitted data/environment; mark synthetic if no real sample is authorized. |
| Produce and deliver kit | `internal` task; `[WORKFLOW_KIT_AGREED]`, `[WORKFLOW_KIT_DELIVERED]` notes | Checked private files; no zero-price quote, receivable, payment, credit or commission for the kit. |
| Review results | `demo` only when actually held; `demo` task with true outcome | Customer inspected results; file delivery alone is not a demo or buyer attendance. |
| Deeper pilot if needed | `pilot`, existing pilot/result fields | Separate agreement, approved price/effort, authorized data and product gate; not another free cycle. |
| Paid proposal | `proposal`; `capabilities_confirmed`, next step; `lumi-solution` quote | Evidence-backed technical confirmation and authorized price approval; a field is not that approval. |
| Acceptance | Accepted quote and `accepted` subject to playbook requirements | Acceptance evidence; **accepted is not paid**. |
| Payment / delivery | Ledger / Finance; handoff / Fulfillment | Finance confirms received money against milestones; Fulfillment records output, checks and acceptance. |
| Stop / pause | `lost` or `on_hold`, reason and permission-based tasks | Do not hold opportunities open on an undated promise. |

**No new automatic stages or fields.** Markers are manual note conventions; kit/data/price gates in this guide are operating checklists, not newly implemented server enforcement. Actual stage requirements come from the opportunity's playbook version. `demo`/`pilot` may be skipped only as the playbook permits, with a reason; never fabricate their completion.

**Paid pilot before the larger implementation:** when pilot acceptance/payment must precede readiness of the main opportunity, use a linked `lumi-solution` opportunity for that pilot buying decision and scope. Do not mark the main implementation won just because a pilot was paid. Do not force stage order to match prose, and deduplicate company-level funnel counts. Do not rewrite seeds, configuration or ledger entries to launch this offer.

## 8. Quotes and payments: free work, services and ARR are different

| Item | How to use pricing sources |
|---|---|
| Workflow Kit | Free within accepted scope; not a 9.9m credit and not a change to Local Trust Kit rights. |
| Agents / BI | Current source lists 9.9m VND once for each implementation item, 19.8m for both; maintenance 6.9m/year or training/improvement at 3 / 6m/month. Scope references, not evidence every connector is ready. |
| Vaults | Pricing remains an **internal proposal, not a live quote**: 9.9m pilot; Team 3.9m/month + 9.9m setup; Company 6.9m/month + 19.9m setup. Do not quote these before commercial and release approval. |
| Third parties / operations | Identify who pays model/API, software, hardware, backup, support, migration, tax and extra labor. Do not add hidden costs behind “free.” |

The governing source and approved quote matter more than copied numbers in this guide. CRM `lumi-solution` is a custom offer, not an automatically installed Agents/Vaults price list. An Admin saves a new offer version when needed; do not alter accepted snapshots or apply changes retroactively.

Retain the previous Agents/BI negotiating outline, **50% at signing and 50% at acceptance**, only where written into agreed terms and technically feasible. Do not automatically apply it to Vaults; pilot and subscription milestones are separately approved. Never take a deposit for an unverified capability. Silence is not acceptance. Finance confirms received money; Partners do not handle payments; no personal payment accounts.

CRM may create a receivable for the full accepted scope. A deposit is a **partial payment**, not payment of the whole contract. Record contractual dates/milestones in terms and tasks; this guide adds no installment engine. Do not create duplicate receivables for the same amount. The kit creates no zero-value payment, revenue, credit or commission.

**Free → paid** requires a new explicit buying decision; nothing activates after a demo automatically. One-time setup/pilot work is not ARR, training is service revenue, and recurring revenue is annualized only from effective contracted recurring terms. Separate pilot and rollout cash, including any expressly approved credit, without double counting.

## 9. Paid-pilot/implementation and handover checklist

Sales, technical staff and authorized customer owners agree the job, sources/destinations, data rights, current tools, scope/revisions, reviewer, environment/model, costs, acceptance criteria, support, tax, cancellation/refunds, export/deletion and decision date. Vaults additionally requires the actual release, server permissions, audit, tested backup/restore, operational ownership and applicable G2/G3 sign-off.

Use existing handoff tools and Fulfillment authority: frozen quote scope, tested deliverables, operating/review instructions, exceptions, limits, data owner and support owner. No customer data in Git; CRM files stay private, with no public sharing links for sensitive data or results. Do not paste secret-bearing documents into notes broadcast to team notifications.

Acceptance requires evidence against agreed criteria and customer confirmation, with owners/dates for remaining defects. Kit acceptance or one demo is not production delivery acceptance. Follow actual repeated use at agreed checkpoints, for example days 7 and 30. Expand/renew based on usage, support burden and value, not the number of running agents.

## 11. Measurement and continuation limits

Start with 10 relevant companies and identified owners, in small batches, not 100 speculative kits. In week one: choose the job, build one synthetic example, invite discussions, accept at most 3 qualified kits and review results. If a capability is not ready, stay in simulation; do not acquire customers with unsupported promises.

Track separately: companies approached → relevant responses → kits agreed → delivered → reviewed → priced proposal → cash-paid pilot → cash-paid implementation → accepted delivery → repeat usage/renewal. Deduplicate company-level conversion; pilot and implementation remain separate transactions. The old 70% charter target, kit receipts and views are **not** paid conversion.

All research, sampling, meetings and lost prospects are acquisition costs: use `research`, `preview`, `sales`, `travel`; contractual delivery uses the corresponding delivery categories. Do not enter zero for unknown labor cost or duplicate task/cost minutes. Local cohort reports do not automatically understand the Workflow Kit or solution funnel; manually reconcile private CRM evidence rather than claim a new dashboard exists.

Primary measure: **realized contribution per total selling + delivery hour**, including a support reserve; expectations are not observations. Illustration only: 10 kits × 2 hours × 350,000 VND/hour = 7,000,000 VND labor, before prospecting/model/implementation. One 9.9m sale does not automatically establish profitability.

Proposed test thresholds, requiring team approval and not benchmarks: after 10 accepted kits that are completed or stopped with a reason, at least 3 result discussions with a buyer or their authorized delegate, at least 1 suitable paid next step with confirmed cash, and a credible positive-contribution delivery path. If product gates remain closed, do not sell merely to hit the target; record the readiness blocker and do not expand.

Stop increasing free work when 2-hour overruns recur, recipients only collect free files, no buyer is reachable, data is unauthorized, or two consecutive cases expose an undeliverable promised capability. Fix ICP/scope or stop, not unlimited free pilots. Validate repeat use/payment before hiring sales. Compare Local and solution on actual cash/effort, not gifts distributed.
