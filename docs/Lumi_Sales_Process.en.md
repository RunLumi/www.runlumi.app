# Lumi sales process — free Basic → owner visit → website + Pro

**Version 2.1 · 17 October 2026 (supersedes 2.0 of 15 October).** Changes in 2.1: a private draft of the business's own page before the visit, walk-in permission for street-front clusters, a factual presence-gap note, one introduction asked after every positive outcome, and a weekly funnel review. No prices, offers, stages or fields change. For the Founder, Sales Manager, Sales Executive, Lumi Partner, Finance and Fulfillment, within their Workspace permissions.

> **Lumi Local:** give a printed Trust Kit Basic, obtain permission to meet the owner, deliver the gift and scan-test it, discover the need, show a website preview, agree Starter with Trust Kit Pro, confirm payment, fulfill and follow up on renewal. **Basic requires no purchase; the website is the main product; Pro is an included deliverable.**

This is the operating guide displayed inside the CRM, not a data-changing action. Saved playbooks, offers and quotes retain their versions. Before quoting physical printing/Pro, an Admin must create an approved offer version with that scope; do not use an older quote saying artwork only. This workflow does not automatically message customers, order printing, collect money, publish sites or pay commission.

## 1. Know the offer before visiting

| Item | What the customer receives | Price and boundaries |
|---|---|---|
| **Trust Kit Basic** | One standard fold-out, self-standing card, the business name, a direct Google review QR, and a file to reprint | **Free.** No website purchase, demo attendance, promotional post, referral or review of Lumi is required. Do not collect payment or create a receivable for the gift. |
| **Lumi Local Starter + Trust Kit Pro** | Mobile website, owner-approved content, Call/Zalo, Maps, enquiry form, year-one hosting/SSL and technical maintenance, one basic revision round; one Pro kit to agreed specifications | **1,990,000 VND for year one** is the reference price; the quote must state tax inclusion/exclusion and any approved additions. Pro is included, not an undisclosed surcharge or a fabricated promotional value. |
| **Trust Kit Pro** | One approved standard counter card and durable holder; website-aligned design; separately labelled direct-review and website QR codes; print and files | Record material, dimensions, quantity, delivery location and date in scope. No assumed NFC, multi-branch coverage, unlimited reprints or Google management. |
| **Local renewal** | Hosting, SSL and scoped technical upkeep; a RunLumi address or one confirmed eligible standard custom domain | **599,000 VND/year** or **999,000 VND/year**, from year two, as a separate decision and quote. Not year-one revenue; no automatic new Pro kit every year. |
| **Previously paid Trust Kit** | The rights in the quote/contract the customer actually purchased | Preserve eligible **399,000 VND** credit within the applicable 30-day terms; the Starter balance is **1,591,000 VND**. A free Basic gift does not create this credit. |

Customers may buy Starter directly without first accepting Basic. A customer who declines the website or demo keeps an already-gifted Basic. Never disable the QR, demand the gift back or use it to pressure a purchase.

The review QR goes directly to Google, without screening for happy customers. The website QR is a separate destination for services and contact; never replace the review link with a Lumi-renewal-dependent destination. Google links can change: do not promise permanent functionality or review visibility.

## 2. Preconditions for a field-sales batch

The Sales Manager selects **one industry, one geographic cluster and up to 10 confirmed initial visits**. This is a test cap, not permission to expand sales headcount. A cohort is 30 businesses in that cluster: the first 10 prove the drafts and the script; the other 20 run only if the first batch passes the §9 review. Do not spread 30 visits over three districts or three industries — the cluster is what makes neighbours' word-of-mouth and travel cost work. Do not mass-print personalized kits before confirming business identity and appointments.

Before ordering print, confirm:

- Approved Basic/Pro samples, specifications and actual cost; a budget for printing, travel, labour, errors and redelivery.
- A sales owner, a website/Pro delivery owner and a payment-confirmation owner; realistic capacity and dates.
- An Admin saves a **new version** of the Starter offer in CRM, retaining the approved price and explicitly including print/Pro. Price changes require authorized approval before pitching. Old snapshots are never edited.
- Tax treatment, first-year address/domain, ownership/transfer, delivery, revisions and cancellation/refund terms in the written scope. Finance confirms invoicing; this guide does not establish tax or refund rules.
- One industry preview, private and labelled draft, with real enquiry collection disabled, **plus one private draft of each confirmed business's own page**, built from information the business already published (Maps listing, page, photos it posted), at most five minutes of human time each (the cohort gate requires an average of at most five) and reviewed before the visit. Before the visit, put the draft's link, version and sources in the `[DRAFT_READY]` note: the `local-preview` playbook shows `preview_url`, `preview_version`, `preview_sources` and `asset_rights` only from `owner_talked`, so copy them into those fields once the opportunity reaches that stage. `asset_rights` stays `placeholder` until the owner supplies or approves assets. A completed website is not required before a need exists; a draft the owner recognizes as their own business is what gets a reaction at the counter.

**Unknown costs or no approved print/Pro offer means the bundle is not ready to quote.** Older Local pages/decks may still exclude printing and stands. Use the approved new scope; do not present conflicting promises. Updating this CRM guide does not update those channels automatically.

## 3. Qualify the business and obtain permission

Prioritize an operating business with in-person customers, no suitable review card, a reachable owner and a specific service/pricing/contact-information problem on mobile. Use evidence of the need, not a Google star rating as a proxy for service quality or website demand.

Create or reuse the company, its contact and **one Local opportunity** in CRM. Do not duplicate the account to record a gift. Check duplicates, opportunity ownership, contact permission and do-not-contact before scheduling.

**Permission-first opener:**

> I’m [Name] from Lumi Local; we build websites for local businesses. We’re offering a printed Google review QR card with your business name and can bring it over and scan-test it with you. You do not need to buy anything. If convenient, may I also show you a relevant website preview for three minutes? You keep the gift even if you decline the demo. When would the owner be available?

Record the decision maker, availability, entrance/location, route cluster and permission. Accepting a gift is not consent to a demo, a purchase or unlimited follow-up. Do not pressure a busy owner into a visit or switch channels after a stop-contact request.

**Walk-in for street-front clusters.** Where businesses sit along one street or market (cafés, salons, spas, small shops), permission may be asked at the counter with the Basic in hand instead of by phone first; use the same words, and a "not now" ends the attempt. Phone or Zalo first remains the rule for garages, HVAC and any business whose owner is rarely at the counter. Record the actual channel in `owner_contact_basis`.

**Introductions outrank cold prospects.** An introduction from a current customer or a neighbouring owner is worked before any cold prospect in the batch. Create the opportunity with the source channel set to referral and the referrer recorded; it still needs `presence_checked` before `qualified`.

**Presence gap, stated as fact.** Before the visit, write a `[PRESENCE_GAP]` note with what is publicly checkable: review count compared with comparable businesses nearby, missing prices, hours or photos, a contact route that does not work. Say what you saw. Never turn it into lost customers, lost revenue or a ranking claim.

## 4. Prepare Basic and check the QR before printing

Have the owner confirm the name, address/branch and review destination. When needed, guide the owner to obtain their review link from their own Google Business Profile. Never request passwords, OTPs or administrative access. A Maps link is not necessarily a write-review link.

Use approved templates and permitted logos/photos; do not invent prices, reviews or outcomes. Basic must be attractive and usable, not deliberately poor to force Pro. Keep Lumi attribution small; do not imply a Google rating, certification or endorsement.

**Invitation on the card:**

> How was your experience today? Scan to share your review on Google. All feedback is welcome.

Check a sample at its actual print size: correct branch, legible contrast, clear margins, no fold through the code, and successful scans on available devices. Record the checker, date, artwork version and confirmed destination. Do not hand over an unverified QR.

Invite genuine customers to give voluntary, honest feedback. No five-star requests, rewards/discounts for reviews, happy-customer-only routing, pressure to review at the counter or test reviews written by Lumi staff. Receiving Basic never requires reviewing Lumi. Google controls publication and visibility; scanning is not proof of a posted review.

## 5. The visit: give → ask → preview → decide

### 5.1 Deliver and scan-test first

Ask permission to place the card, scan-test it with the owner and explain an honest review invitation. The gift is independent of the purchase. If an employee receives it, record the actual recipient; do not mark an owner conversation. An absent owner is a visit outcome, and a return appointment requires consent.

### 5.2 Ask about one real friction

> When customers ask about your services, prices or quotation process, do you send several pictures or already have one link? When did a customer last have to ask again or find the wrong information?

Existing channels may already be sufficient. Wanting an attractive QR card does not prove website demand. Do not claim lost customers without evidence.

### 5.3 Ask to show the preview

> This QR card serves people who have already visited. For someone still choosing, I prepared a draft page for [business name] from what is already public: services, hours, directions, then Call or Zalo. Thirty seconds on your phone — tell me what is wrong.

Show the business's own draft whenever one exists; the industry sample is the fallback, not the default. After the first paid sites in a cluster, a live customer site may be shown as the example only with that customer's recorded permission. Corrections the owner makes at the counter are the first content round: note them in `owner_feedback`. Use one relevant mobile preview and one scope/price page, not the entire deck at the counter. Use confirmed details or labelled placeholders. No live enquiry capture, domain purchase or official publication without approval.

### 5.4 Agree the next step; do not sell through obligation

For a fit, explain Starter, exact Pro deliverables, first-year and renewal prices, and delivery terms. Ask whether they are ready to confirm scope or what remains unclear. No fake deadlines or assumption that receiving a gift means buying.

For undecided customers, record the objection and **agreed** next step. For a decline or poor fit, record the truth and respect the stop request. Do not increase gift value to compensate for absent website value.

**Ask for one introduction** after any positive outcome — a gift accepted, a draft corrected or a purchase — and never as a condition of either. Record it as a `[REFERRAL]` note with the name and whether the owner allows being mentioned; the new opportunity carries the referral source. No payment, discount or gift for introductions unless an approved offer version says so.

## 6. Use the existing CRM, not a parallel pipeline

Run the Basic/Pro actions using the **existing `local-preview` playbook**, tasks, notes, costs, quotes and fulfillment. This guide adds no database stages or fields.

| Actual event | Where it goes | Requirement and non-inference |
|---|---|---|
| Research a business | `new`; industry, location, Maps and website fields | A missing website alone does not establish a buying need. |
| Qualified to approach | `qualified`; satisfy that playbook version’s fields/checklist | No owner contact has occurred merely because the prospect is qualified. |
| Owner agrees to a visit | `visit` task, date/time/contact, appointment confirmation; note gift and demo consent separately | Booking is not a completed owner conversation. |
| Prepare/print Basic | `internal` task; `[BASIC_READY]` note with file, QR target and checker; log cost | No Basic quote, payment or commission. |
| Prepare the business's own draft | `internal` task; `[DRAFT_READY]` note with the draft's link, version and sources; minutes under `preview`; copied into `preview_url`, `preview_version`, `preview_sources` at `owner_talked` | A draft is not a conversation and never becomes a public site without the owner's approval. |
| Introduction received | New opportunity on the referred account with the referral source and referrer; `[REFERRAL]` note on the referring opportunity | An introduction is not a qualified lead until `presence_checked`. |
| Meet owner and give Basic | Complete `visit` with its true outcome; `[BASIC_DELIVERED]` or `[BASIC_DECLINED]` note | Move to `owner_talked` only after talking to the decision maker and meeting the playbook requirements. |
| Owner absent / reschedule | `owner_absent`, `rescheduled`, `no_show` or `declined`, as observed | An employee receiving the gift is not an owner conversation or a new lead. |
| Show website | `preview_shown`; version, sources, asset permissions and feedback | Optional steps can be skipped with a recorded reason; a demo is not required to keep the gift. |
| Decide and quote | `deciding`; next step/date and correct offer version | Unknown value remains blank, not zero. |
| Customer accepts | Accepted quote with evidence, then `accepted` when requirements pass | **Accepted is not paid.** Do not convert a gifted Basic into 399,000 VND credit. |
| Receive funds | Seller reports within permission; **Finance confirms** ledger payment | A transfer screenshot/customer statement does not replace Finance confirmation. Partners do not handle money. |
| Deliver website and Pro | Handoff + Fulfillment; Pro scope, artwork version, QR checks and receipt evidence | `[PRO_DELIVERED]` is a note, not an automatic fulfillment transition or commission trigger. |
| Renew | New `local-renewal` opportunity linked by `parent_id` to the same account | Do not duplicate the customer or book renewal revenue early. |

`[BASIC_READY]`, `[BASIC_DELIVERED]`, `[BASIC_DECLINED]`, `[PRO_DELIVERED]`, `[DRAFT_READY]`, `[PRESENCE_GAP]` and `[REFERRAL]` are **manual note conventions**, not automation. Do not claim automated Basic/Pro counts. Reconcile through history; contact details and customer imagery stay in private CRM/files, never in Git.

## 7. Quotes, money and existing customer rights

Give Basic before and independently of payment. For **paid Local delivery**, retain payment in full under the agreed scope before official publication and Pro handover. Any exception needs authorized, documented approval.

**Minimum written confirmation:**

```text
LUMI LOCAL STARTER + TRUST KIT PRO — Opportunity: [CRM ID]
Business / authorized representative: [...]
Offer ID and version: [...]
Year one: 1,990,000 VND; confirmed tax and separately charged items: [...]
Eligible credit from a PREVIOUSLY PAID Trust Kit: [...]
Amount payable: [...]; gifted Basic is not credit.
Website: approved content, Call/Zalo, Maps, enquiry form, hosting/SSL,
  year-one technical maintenance and one basic content revision.
Pro: [material, dimensions, quantity, holder, two QR destinations, files].
Delivery location / actual agreed date: [...]
Year-one domain/address, ownership, transfer and exclusions: [...]
Year-two renewal: 599,000 or 999,000 VND/year for the confirmed option.
Not included by default: ads, Google profile management, online store,
  booking, CRM, chatbot, unlimited edits/reprints or multiple branches.
No promise of review volume, rankings, customers or revenue.
Payment and cancellation/refund terms as agreed: [...]
Acceptance evidence / invoicing details: [...]
```

Use only company payment details verified by Finance; no cash collection or personal accounts. Recording payment is not the CRM executing it. Invoicing, tax and cancellation/refunds follow valid agreements and Finance review, not sales assumptions.

Customers who previously paid 399,000 VND retain eligible 30-day upgrade rights under their snapshots and payment history. Never delete or retrospectively withdraw earned credit. Record payments, credits, refunds and voids using the existing append-only functions. Basic is not 399,000 VND collected, a fictitious discount or commission.

## 8. Fulfill the website and Pro; follow up

Sales hands over the accepted quote, payment information for Finance reconciliation, owner-confirmed services/prices/hours, asset permission, domain ownership, enquiry recipient, revision scope, delivery date, Pro specifications and unresolved information.

Fulfillment verifies and records evidence:

- The owner approved the final site; Call/Zalo open the correct destinations, Maps to the confirmed location, enquiries to the business’s designated recipient, and HTTPS/mobile work.
- Pro matches approved name/logo/artwork. Scan the direct-review QR and the separate authorized live-site QR again after printing.
- The print, holder and files were delivered in the agreed quantity; record actual recipient/date/receipt. Obtain permission before using a customer/person/counter image for marketing.
- Record defects and an owner for resolution. Do not mark acceptance while committed website or Pro work remains missing. CRM currently has no separate Pro status: use handoff, check notes and acceptance evidence; only Fulfillment-authorized users update delivery.

Agree a useful follow-up date. Ask whether the card is in use or links need correction, without demanding photos or reviews. Renewal is separate. Propose Lumi Ops/Agents only for a real recurring operational need, not as a condition of Basic or the website.

## 9. Costs, measurement and stop conditions

**Basic gifts are not revenue.** Observe the entire cohort: qualified businesses → agreed visits → owners actually met → Basic received/used → website preview → paid Starter → complete, contribution-positive delivery.

Classify costs by purpose in the existing CRM:

| Cost | Existing category | Recording guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Free Basic: paper, printing, template work, packaging | `sales` | Add `trust-kit-basic/print` or `/prepare`, evidence and quantity so it is included in acquisition cost. |
| Travel, including owner absences | `travel` | Record the route cluster and allocate consistently or use a cohort-level entry; never double count. |
| Research, preview and selling | `research`, `preview`, `sales` | Include founder time and unsuccessful opportunities. |
| Pro included in a sold Starter | `printing`, `delivery` | Fulfillment cost; record specifications, quantity and redelivery effort. |
| Website, domain, hosting and support | Matching category | Separate `actual` from `estimate`; support reserve is not cash already spent. |

Current reporting puts `printing` in delivery cost, **not CAC**. Therefore Basic uses `sales`; Pro uses `printing`. Do not relabel history to improve metrics. Minutes without a costed amount mean incomplete costing; never enter zero merely to pass a gate. Do not duplicate minutes from task completion in another cost entry.

**Starter CAC = all cohort acquisition costs / unique paid Starter businesses.** Year-one contribution also subtracts website/Pro delivery, domain/hosting, refunds and support reserve. Do not justify current losses using unearned renewals.

Illustration, not Lumi actuals: if reaching 30 businesses costs 2,700,000 VND, two Starters imply 1,350,000 VND CAC; five imply 540,000; nine imply 300,000. Website and Pro delivery remain extra. Two wins show a learning signal, not a profitable channel.

Internal hypotheses for another bounded cohort: average human preview work at most five minutes, CAC aiming at no more than 600,000 VND/Starter, standardized fulfillment and positive contribution after reserve. These are not achieved results. Existing cohort/commission gates remain governed by their stored policy versions; this guide does not mutate them.

**Weekly funnel review** on one line of figures: own drafts prepared → owners attempted → owners met → Basic delivered → own draft shown → paid Starter → introductions received. For a cluster of 30 the working target is 30 drafts and 30 owner attempts inside two weeks; a batch that cannot reach owners is a targeting or timing problem, not a pitch problem, and gets a different cluster or visiting hour, not more gift. Compare own-draft visits with industry-sample visits on paid Starters per owner met.

Pause increased printing/visits when the owner is unreachable, QR use is wrong, costs are unknown, gifts are welcomed but websites are not needed, or delivery loses money. Do not fix poor fit by making the gift more expensive. If 20 qualified owner conversations produce no paid Starter, revisit the need/segment/pitch: the first thing to try is leading with the presence gap and the review card, with the website as the attached deliverable, before changing price or gift. After the first cohort, compare a preview-only visit with Basic-assisted visits; small samples provide directional evidence only.

## 10. Short counter-side scripts

**Give Basic:**

> This card is yours. Let’s check that the scan opens the right business. You do not have to buy a website or review Lumi to keep it.

**Walk-in opener (street-front cluster):**

> Hello, I'm [Name] from Lumi Local. We're giving businesses on this street a Google review card with their own name — this one is yours; let's scan it to check it opens the right place. You don't need to buy anything. Is the owner here, or when is a good time?

**Invite a preview:**

> Do you already have one link for customers to see your services and contact you? If you are sending several pictures instead, may I show a draft on a phone?

**Show the business's own draft:**

> I made a draft for [business name] from what's already on Maps. It's private, nothing is published. What's wrong on it — prices, hours, anything missing?

**Ask for one introduction:**

> Do you know one other owner nearby who would want the same card? May I say you spoke well of it? No obligation either way.

**Explain the approved bundle:**

> Starter is 1,990,000 VND for year one. This agreed scope includes the website and Pro kit [specifications]. Year-two renewal is [599,000 or 999,000 VND/year for the confirmed option]. I will itemize the domain, tax and exclusions before you decide.

**Owner unavailable:**

> I understand this is not convenient. Would you like another agreed time? Otherwise, I will not keep following up.

**No website need / decline:**

> Please keep using the card. If your existing channels already explain everything clearly, you do not need another website now.

**Handover:**

> Here are the website, files and Pro kit under scope [ID]. Let’s check both QR codes, Call/Zalo and your business details. I will record any unresolved items; the maintenance period and renewal terms are [agreed terms].

## 11. Agents / BI stays a separate motion

The Basic/Pro change applies to **Lumi Local**, not Agents/BI pricing or existing commitments. Use `lumi-solution`; Vaults or a storage migration is not a mandatory prerequisite.

Retain discovery of one recurring task, authorized data, a reviewer and budget owner. Confirm feasibility before real-data processing or a deposit. A free evaluation needs bounded scope, staff-time budget, success criteria and a decision date. The existing planning cap is two calendar weeks and two staff-days, not a universal delivery promise. Synthetic figures are not customer results.

Reference figures from existing materials: BI setup 9.9 million VND; Agents setup 9.9 million; both 19.8 million; Basic maintenance 6.9 million/year; training/improvements 3 or 6 million/month. Confirm current approved scope, quote version and deliverability; itemize third-party costs. One-time implementation is not ARR.

Retain the agreed contractual framework of 50% at signing and 50% at acceptance, with approved exceptions. Finance confirms money; Fulfillment records results and acceptance evidence. Do not invent deemed acceptance through silence, SLAs, refunds or tax treatment without reviewed terms. Customer case studies and referrals require appropriate permission and supported outcomes.
