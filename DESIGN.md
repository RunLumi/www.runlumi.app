# Lumi Landing Design

## Idea

**Civic Editorial Intelligence**: a calm operating document for a company entering the AI era.

The unconventional move is restraint. The page should feel closer to a beautifully edited annual report, operating manual, or systems map than a conventional AI startup page.

## Brand contract

- Paper White: `#F4F0E8`
- Surface White: `#FFFFFF`
- Civic Navy: `#102A43`
- Ink: `#172033`
- Lumi Blue: `#006093`
- Blue soft: `#E4F3FC`
- Slate: `#5E6677`
- Border: `#D9DEE8`
- Signal Amber: `#F4A62A`
- Risk Red: `#C2410C`
- Success Green: `#1F7A4D`

Light-only. No dark hero. Use Geist when available, then system sans. Headlines are large, tight, and authoritative.

## Landing grammar

1. Vision: a large idea, not a screenshot collage.
2. Stack: architecture is the product story.
3. Day in the company: show products working together.
4. Company context: explain shared intelligence.
5. Products: editorial chapters, not equal SaaS cards.
6. Existing stack: Lumi augments rather than replaces.
7. Trust: evidence, permissions, approvals, auditability.
8. CTA: a concrete conversation, not urgency theatre.

## Material

Warm paper canvas, white sheets, thin archival borders, navy-tinted shadows, blue as authority, modest radius, and folded-L-inspired line geometry.

No decorative gradients except subtle material lighting. No glass marketing cards.

## Motion

No video required. Keep motion functional: 120–180ms hover/focus, 1px lifts, and full reduced-motion support.

## Copy

Use specific nouns and verbs.

Good: “Ask why margin fell.” “Trace the answer back to orders.” “Give the follow-up to an agent.”

Avoid: “Transform your enterprise with next-generation agentic intelligence.”
